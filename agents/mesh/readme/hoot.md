# Seance · Dr. Hoot

**Address:** `agent1qf84m8mamqfjn49qutwucdtez4clrwavq4nzqlnw37hramsnk8hwzlthq5v`

The skeptic of **SEANCE**, a fidelity court for resurrected web apps. When someone claims a dead app is back,
Dr. Hoot tries to prove the revival is a fake.

## Who is Dr. Hoot

A grumpy owl in round spectacles with a stopwatch. Nothing has ever impressed him. Before anyone testifies he
seals his edge-case attacks (empty input, double operators, divide by zero, a 500-character todo item) in hashed
envelopes, so nobody can say he aimed them after the fact. His attacks come only from a deterministic skeptic: no
model writes, picks or edits one. His banter is ASI-1. He says "Hoo." a lot.

## Commands

| Command | Example | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `attacks` | `attacks todo` | how many attacks I sealed, per screen |
| `seal` | `seal todo` | the seal hashes, checked against the seal committed to git |
| `reveal` | `reveal todo` | the attacks that caught the revival, only after a ruling |
| `help` | `help` | the command card |
| anything else | `is the revival any good?` | banter in character, with the case's real numbers |
